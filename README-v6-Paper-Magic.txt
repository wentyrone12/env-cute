NEW FEATURES
  ✓ Premium Typewriter: message reveals left-to-right one character at a time.
  ✓ Adjustable speed: Gentle, Classic, Quick.
  ✓ 3D paper page-turn with realistic shadow, fold, and backside.
  ✓ Next, Back, and swipe navigation; buttons lock during page turning.
  ✓ Show full text: skip typing without leaving the page.
  ✓ Animations saved with the envelope and seen by shared-link recipients.
  ✓ Compatible with Reduce Motion and mobile layouts.
  ✓ Existing v5 24 photo borders, 24 photo layouts, and admin analytics kept.

TO USE
  1) Run on localhost/HTTPS and sign in with a Premium-approved account.
  2) Open Create. Find Paper Magic above Your letter pages.
  3) Switch on Live typewriter and 3D page turns; select typing speed.
  4) Make two or more pages, click Seal, then View to test the effects.
  5) Click Next / Back or swipe across the paper on your phone.

TIPS
  • New envelopes saved by Free accounts do not get premium animations.
  • Recipients can see paper effects without being Premium subscribers.
  • For older envelopes, open Edit and Save with Paper Magic enabled to
    update existing shared links (if existing permissions allow updates).
  • Respect Reduced Motion: animations turn off for that reader.
  • Replace the full site files on hosting and hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R).
